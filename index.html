Questions

The things people ask before they call.

What does the snapshot cost?

Nothing. Send us your address and we send back the zoning, a yield range and the three best uses for your land within 48 hours.

How much is the full appraisal?

$4,950 plus GST, fixed. If you go ahead with us within 90 days, every dollar comes off our delivery fees.

Do I keep control of my land?

Yes. You approve every stage before we move to the next one. It's your site and your decision, always.

What if the numbers don't stack up?

Then you find out in 10 days, not two years. The appraisal also shows your other options: sell with an approval, joint venture, or hold. If it can't give you a clear answer, you get your money back.

I have capital but no site. Can you help?

Yes. We source and appraise sites to your brief, and we can structure joint ventures with landowners.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Townhouses, co-living, build-to-rent, health and mixed-use, across Queensland from our base in Cairns.

How are you paid for delivery?

A management fee on development cost. You hold 20% of it until we land your project on budget and program. We earn a bonus only if we beat the feasibility.
